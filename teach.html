Teach the impressive
thing first.

This is the 20-minute segment where new members get real leads on day one. Teach it like a magic trick, not like a lesson. This page is your run sheet: timings, say-lines, and the stalls you will hit.

The idea · 60 seconds of theory, maximum

Why this works

Most programs open with setup and theory, and the good stuff arrives in week four. We flip it. The member ships one real post in their first sitting and watches comments arrive the same day. THEN they want the foundations. The post itself never changes shape:

I just made THING for PERSON who wants RESULT in TIMEFRAME. One line that sounds like you. Want a copy? Drop KEYWORD in the comments.

Every comment with the keyword is a lead. Delivery is by email, so every copy handed out is a subscriber. The snack sells the meal.

The frame · teach it as a magic trick

Three moves, in this order

MOVE 1 · THE PLEDGE
Show something ordinary

Their existing list and one plain message. Nothing up your sleeve. No funnel, no ads, no landing page.

MOVE 2 · THE TURN
Something amazing, live

AI turns their rough sentence into a post that sounds like them on their best day. Do it on screen, in front of everyone.

MOVE 3 · THE PRESTIGE
Something comes back

Comments and DMs land the same day. Leads out of nowhere, before onboarding even finishes.

The run sheet · 20 minutes, live

Minute by minute

0 – 2
The pledge, on your own screen

Open the Post Writer. Show YOUR example first (the See an Example button). Let them see a finished one before they build.

Watch my screen first, then you do it. Look, it is just your existing list and one plain message. That is all this is.
2 – 5
The five blanks, explained fast

Thing, person, result, timeframe, keyword. One sentence each. The person must be so specific they say "that's me".

Not "business owners". Say the person so exactly that one human reading it goes: that is me. That is the whole trick.
5 – 10
They fill their five blanks

Timer on screen. CSMs watch the chat for anyone stuck on "the thing". The fix is always something they already do with clients.

Five minutes on the clock. Your thing already exists, it is something you already do with clients. Do not invent, shrink. Ready, set, go.
10 – 13
The turn: AI writes it

Everyone hits the write button together. Three variants come back plus the replies. Tell them to read variant 2 out loud quietly.

Hit the button. While it writes, understand this: it is using YOUR words from the blanks. Pick the variant that sounds most like you on a good day.
13 – 17
The prestige: they post, now

Not after the call. Now. Doubled check-in: who posted, who posted? Celebrate every one in chat by name.

Copy it. Open your platform. Press post. Yes, now, during the call. Posted beats perfect. Type POSTED in the chat when it is live.
17 – 20
Arm them with the replies

Show the Replies tab: delivery, thanks, real question, day-3 silence. Delivery reply always asks for the email.

Comments are coming tonight. Your four replies are already written, in the Replies tab. Reply one always gets the email. The email IS the lead.
The stalls · you will hit these

Common stalls and the fix

What you hearWhat to say
"I do not have a thing to give away"You do. What do you repeat with every client in week one? That checklist, that question list, that template. Shrink it, do not create it.
"My audience is tiny"This needs ten real humans, not ten thousand. Use the DM variant: send it to ten warm people directly. Same trick, no stage fright.
"It does not sound like me"Change three words, not thirty. Then post. Your Second Brain fixes voice permanently next week, today we ship.
"Can I post tomorrow instead?"No. Posted beats perfect, and the room is posting with you right now. Tomorrow is where posts go to die.
"What if nobody comments?"Then send the DM variant to ten people tonight. Hand-raisers work on the second and third try too. You now own the machine.
After the call · the follow-through

What happens tonight

Members reply to every keyword comment with reply 1 (ask for the email, plus one small question). Deliver by email. Day 3 quiet? Reply 4: give one new useful thing, zero guilt. Pitch only when someone asks what you do. Celebrate every lead in the community, by name, loudly.
